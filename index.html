Free online darts scoring app

Score X01 games on standard and Quadro dartboards

Quadscorer is a quick, phone-friendly darts scorer for 301, 501, 701 and custom X01 games. It works with ordinary dartboards and Quadro boards with a quadruple scoring ring.

A darts scorer built for Quadro boards

Most darts scoring apps stop at singles, doubles and trebles. Quadro mode adds Q1 to Q20, accepts visits up to 240 and calculates checkout routes that use the quadruple ring.

Fast X01 score entry

Enter the visit total with the compact keypad or record each dart separately. Quadscorer checks legal three-dart totals, applies double-out rules and catches busts automatically.

Install it on your phone

Add Quadscorer to your home screen and use it like a standalone darts scoring app. Games are saved on the device, restored after a refresh and available without a network connection.

No account or subscription

There is no sign-up and no league-management clutter. Start a two-player game, score the darts and use undo or visit history when a score needs correcting.

Quadro darts scoring questions

What is a Quadro dartboard?

A Quadro dartboard adds a fourth scoring ring. A dart in that ring scores four times the segment number, so Q20 scores 80 and a three-dart visit can reach 240.

Can I use Quadscorer for a normal dartboard?

Yes. Choose Standard when starting the game and the scorer uses normal single, double, treble and bull values.

Does the scorer support 301 and 501?

Yes. It includes quick choices for 301, 501 and 701, plus a custom starting score for other X01 games.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit, the installable web app can load offline and keeps the current game in local storage on the device.
